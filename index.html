Transport Zones

Structured, upfront travel pricing

Signature operates across Cape Town and surrounding areas — Southern Suburbs, the CBD, Atlantic Seaboard, Northern Suburbs, stadiums, venues and festival locations. Travel costs are always confirmed before you book, wherever your event is.

Zone 1

No transport fee
  • Retreat
  • Grassy Park
  • Steenberg
  • Lavender Hill
  • Tokai & surrounds

Free transport, included in the service price.

Zone 2

Small transport fee
  • Wynberg
  • Plumstead
  • Claremont
  • Muizenberg
  • Constantia
  • Observatory

A nominal fee is added per staff member.

Zone 3

Full transport fee
  • Cape Town CBD
  • Sea Point

  • Milnerton
  • Bloubergstrand

Arranged via Bolt/Uber, charged in full.

For staff safety, events ending after 8:00 PM always include arranged transport, regardless of zone. All transport costs are confirmed upfront and included in your final quote.

Late-night transport: A surcharge may apply to staff transport required after midnight. Any applicable surcharge will be confirmed upfront with your quotation. See our full Terms & Conditions for details.
