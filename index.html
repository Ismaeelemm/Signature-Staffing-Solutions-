Transport Zones

Structured, upfront travel pricing

We operate across Cape Town with a zoned transport system, so travel costs are always confirmed before you book — never a surprise on the day.

Zone 1

No transport fee
  • Retreat
  • Grassy Park
  • Steenberg
  • Lavender Hill
  • Tokai & surrounds

Free transport, included in the service price.

Zone 2

Small transport fee
  • Wynberg
  • Plumstead
  • Claremont
  • Muizenberg
  • Constantia
  • Observatory

A nominal fee is added per staff member.

Zone 3

Full transport fee
  • Cape Town CBD
  • Sea Point
  • Camps Bay
  • Milnerton
  • Bloubergstrand
  • Northern Suburbs

Arranged via Bolt/Uber, charged in full.

Events ending after 8:00 PM always require transport, regardless of zone, to ensure staff safety. All transport costs are confirmed upfront and included in your final quote — you can also choose to arrange transport for staff yourself.
