Booking Conditions

Terms & Conditions

The essentials for securing and running your booking smoothly.

Deposit Policy

A non-refundable 50% deposit is required to secure your staff booking and lock in the date on our calendar.

Balance Payment

The remaining 50% balance must be settled via EFT 48 hours before the team departs the hub. No staff will leave the hub if the balance is outstanding.

Overtime Clause

If the event runs over the specified 6 hours, additional time is billed at each staff member's normal booked hourly rate plus R50 per hour, per staff member, rounded up to the nearest 30 minutes.

Staff Welfare

For shifts exceeding 5 hours, clients are kindly requested to provide staff with basic meals and drinking water.
