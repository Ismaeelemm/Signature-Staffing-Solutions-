01. General Terms

These Terms & Conditions apply to all services provided by Signature Staffing Solutions ("Signature", "we", "us"). By requesting or booking our services, the client agrees to be bound by these terms.

An enquiry or quotation does not, on its own, constitute a confirmed booking. Signature may update these Terms & Conditions from time to time; the version applicable to a booking is the version accepted at the time that booking is confirmed.

02. Services

Signature provides trained event staff, including:

  • Waiters
  • Bar staff
  • Runners
  • Hosts / event assistants
  • Setup and breakdown crews
  • Cleaning / scullery staff
  • General event support
  • Tailored staffing solutions built around your requirements

The exact services supplied for any booking are determined by the client's quotation and confirmed booking.

03. Events We Service

Signature provides staffing for a wide range of occasions, including corporate events, conferences, brand activations and promotional events, weddings and private parties, sporting and stadium events, festivals and wine festivals, gala dinners, cocktail events, and other private or commercial functions. This list is not exhaustive — get in touch if your event isn't listed here.

04. Quotations & Pricing

Quotes are prepared based on the information supplied by the client at the time of enquiry. Changes to guest numbers, staffing requirements, event duration, location or duties may affect the final price.

A quotation does not automatically guarantee staff availability. Transport may be charged separately where applicable, and public holidays, unusual hours, extended hours or special event requirements may attract additional charges. Final pricing is confirmed before a booking is accepted.

05. Booking Confirmation

An enquiry is not a booking. A quotation is not a booking. A booking is only confirmed once Signature has confirmed it and any required deposit or payment has been received. Staff availability is not guaranteed until a booking is confirmed.

Signature may decline or refuse a booking at its discretion where it cannot reasonably fulfil the requirements.

06. Deposits & Payments

Signature may require a deposit to secure a booking — typically a 50% deposit. The exact deposit amount and payment deadline will be stated on the client's quotation or invoice, and the remaining balance is due according to the agreed payment terms.

Late payments may affect Signature's ability to provide the agreed service. Additional services or hours are billed separately, and payment methods will be communicated during the booking process.

07. Cancellations & Postponements

Client-initiated cancellations, postponements, or changes to the event date should be communicated to Signature as soon as possible. Deposits and payments already made may be subject to the cancellation terms agreed at booking, particularly where the cancellation occurs close to the event date and costs have already been incurred.

A postponed event is not automatically treated as a new confirmed booking until the new date has been accepted by Signature. These terms operate subject to applicable South African consumer law.

08. Minimum Booking Period

The standard minimum booking is 4 hours, unless otherwise agreed. Certain packages or special bookings may carry a different minimum — the confirmed minimum for your booking will be shown on the quotation.

09. Overtime & Extended Hours

If an event runs beyond the confirmed booking period, additional time is billed at each staff member's normal booked hourly rate plus R50 per hour, per staff member, rounded up to the nearest 30 minutes.

Overtime should be agreed with the client where reasonably possible. Clients should not assume staff will remain on-site indefinitely without additional payment being agreed.

10. Staffing Numbers

Recommended staffing numbers are based on the event details and service style provided. The staffing calculator on our website provides an estimate only and is not a binding quotation — final staffing numbers are determined during the quotation process.

Large, complex, high-volume or specialist events may require additional staff, and staffing ratios may differ depending on the nature of the event.

11. Staff Roles & Scope of Work

Staff are assigned according to the roles booked for an event. Waiters, bar staff, runners, setup crews, cleaners and other roles carry different responsibilities, and staff should not automatically be expected to perform duties outside the agreed scope of their role. Additional duties may require additional staff or attract additional charges.

12. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing accurate information regarding:

  • Event date and time
  • Venue
  • Guest numbers
  • Type of event
  • Service requirements
  • Setup requirements
  • Bar requirements
  • Access times and parking/access arrangements
  • Any unusual conditions or special requirements

Where any of this information changes, the client must notify Signature as soon as reasonably possible.

13. Venue Access & Delays

Staff must be given reasonable access to the venue at the agreed arrival time. Delays caused by the client, the venue, or circumstances outside Signature's control may affect the service provided, and waiting time or extended hours arising from such delays may attract additional charges where applicable. Setup time must be agreed in advance.

14. Setup & Breakdown

Setup and breakdown services cover the tasks specifically agreed in the booking. Unless specifically quoted for, staff should not be expected to perform dangerous work, specialist construction, electrical work, heavy or hazardous lifting, technical installations, or any work outside their agreed role.

15. Bar Services & Alcohol

Signature provides trained bar staff — not necessarily the alcohol itself. Responsibility for supplying alcohol, obtaining any required licensing, and meeting venue requirements remains with the client or venue, unless specifically agreed otherwise.

Staff may refuse unsafe, unlawful or inappropriate requests, and should not knowingly serve guests where doing so would create an unreasonable safety or legal concern. Signature does not take responsibility for a client's liquor licence or venue compliance unless specifically contracted to do so.

16. Client & Staff Conduct

Signature staff must be treated respectfully at all times. Harassment, intimidation, threats, discrimination, violence or abuse toward staff will not be tolerated, and Signature may remove staff from an unsafe situation or terminate services where serious safety or conduct issues arise.

Signature staff are, in turn, expected to conduct themselves professionally while representing the company.

17. Health, Safety & Unsafe Conditions

Signature aims to provide staff with a safe working environment. Clients and venues must disclose any known hazards relevant to the event. Staff may refuse tasks that are reasonably considered dangerous or outside their competence, and Signature may withdraw staff from an unsafe environment. Emergency situations take priority over normal service obligations.

18. Transport

Transport charges may apply depending on the event location and booking requirements, and will be confirmed as part of the quotation. Staff transport may be arranged by Signature or by the client, as agreed.

Multiple vehicles may be required depending on staff numbers, and unusual waiting time or travel requirements may result in additional charges. See our Pricing Reference below for standard regional travel fees.

19. Late-Night Transport Surcharge

A late-night transport surcharge may apply where Signature is required to provide staff transport after midnight. The applicable surcharge will be confirmed upfront in the quotation, and is based on factors such as the transport requirement, time, distance, number of vehicles and operating circumstances at the time.

20. Client-Arranged Transport

Clients may arrange their own transport for staff where this is agreed in advance. Any client-arranged transport must be safe and suitable for the number of staff travelling, and Signature may decline transport arrangements that appear unsafe or unsuitable. Where Signature is not providing the transport, Signature's transport surcharge does not apply.

21. Staff Availability & Replacements

Signature makes reasonable efforts to provide the agreed number of staff for a booking. Individual staff members are not guaranteed unless specifically agreed. If a staff member becomes unavailable, Signature will aim to provide a suitable replacement wherever reasonably possible, in order to maintain the agreed level of service.

22. Equipment & Client Property

Staff may work with venue or client equipment and property in the course of an event. Clients and venues remain responsible for providing suitable equipment, unless Signature has specifically agreed to supply it. Damage caused by misuse, negligence, or use outside the agreed scope may be dealt with separately.

As Signature's services expand to include equipment such as glassware, linen, bar equipment, tables and chairs, further equipment-hire terms will apply to those specific services.

23. Photographs & Marketing

Signature may request permission to use photographs or video from an event for marketing purposes. Signature does not claim automatic rights to use images of identifiable guests — where appropriate, permission or consent will be sought before using identifiable individuals in marketing material.

Signature may contact a reviewer to verify their review. Approval of a review does not mean Signature agrees with every statement it contains.

Clients should contact Signature as soon as reasonably possible if a service issue arises, giving Signature a reasonable opportunity to investigate and resolve the matter. Complaints should include relevant details such as the event date, venue, and nature of the issue. Signature will aim to resolve legitimate complaints fairly and professionally.
